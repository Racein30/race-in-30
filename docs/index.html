Race in 30

Every race, in thirty seconds.

Race in 30 is an unofficial fan project that turns publicly available motorsport timing data into short animated data videos, posted to the Race in 30 YouTube channel.

The Race in 30 uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload these videos to their own YouTube channel.
